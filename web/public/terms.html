What Phoenical is

Phoenical is a free, open project built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. It turns MODIS and VIIRS active-fire detections from NASA FIRMS into one consistent burning-activity record. Using the site, its exports and its API is free. An account is optional.

About the data

  • Values are fire-days: grid cells with at least one satellite fire detection on a day. They measure burning activity, not burned area or individual fires.
  • Months before 2012 are modelled estimates converted from MODIS, not detections, and are labelled as such.
  • Recent data can change as NASA replaces near-real-time detections with final data.
  • The site and its alerts are not an emergency warning system. Don't rely on them for safety decisions; follow your local civil defence and fire services.

The data is provided "as is", without warranties of accuracy, completeness or availability. Fire data: NASA FIRMS (please credit NASA FIRMS and Phoenical when you reuse exports).

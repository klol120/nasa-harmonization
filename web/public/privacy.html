Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Phoenical shows a harmonized record of satellite fire detections (NASA FIRMS, MODIS and VIIRS). You can use the map, charts and exports without an account and without giving us any personal data. This page explains what we store when you choose the optional extras: signing in, joining the email list, and saving areas.

What we store

When you…We storeWhy
Sign in with GoogleYour email address, name, profile picture link and Google account ID. A sign-in session (we keep only a fingerprint of it, not the session itself).To know it's you and show your saved areas.
Save an areaThe area's name and shape, its region, and your alert settings.To reopen the area and send alerts you asked for.
Join the email listYour email address, the topics you chose, where you signed up, and when you confirmed or unsubscribed.To send the monthly digest and project news.
Receive an email from usA copy of the email and when it was sent.To avoid duplicates and fix delivery problems.

We don't sell or share this data, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't use tracking or analytics cookies. Your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

Google sign-in

When you click "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We don't ask for access to anything else in your Google account. Phoenical's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services the site uses

  • Microsoft Azure (France Central) hosts the website, the API and the database.
  • Map tiles come from CARTO, Esri, OpenStreetMap and Amazon Web Services (terrain); your browser loads them directly, so those providers see your IP address like any website you visit.
  • Google provides the sign-in button and the web fonts.
  • Anthropic runs the assistant (the chat button), when it is switched on. The question you type and a summary of the view on screen (region, month, totals) are sent to Anthropic to write the answer. We don't save the conversation, and you don't need an account to use it. Please don't type personal information into it.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the digest with the link in each digest email, or from your account menu.
  • Turn alerts off for any saved area under "My areas".
  • Delete your account from the account menu. This removes your account, sessions and saved areas, and takes you off the email list straight away.

How long we keep data

Account data stays until you delete your account. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days. If you unsubscribe from the email list, we keep only your address marked as unsubscribed, so we don't email you again.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: privacy@phoenical.earth.
